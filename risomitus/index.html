INK

These values are added on top of the global settings.

Tonal Gives the strong look — flat areas, like a poster. Full gamut Builds a table of every colour these inks can reach and maps each pixel to the nearest combination; A separation faithful to the source colour, so it comes out softer. Raise saturation to push it toward the edges of the gamut.

3Screen

A thermal master cannot burn a hole small enough for the faintest tone, and the darkest tones plug — the tonal range stops at both ends. One of the surest signs of a real print.

4Machine defects

Master irregularity varies each screen cell on its own — without it the dots are too perfect and it reads as digital.

5Paper

Wet ink cockles the paper and the sheet stretches a little between passes — which is why misregistration grows the further you get from the centre.

6Frame by frame

The number is burned into one drum's master, so it takes the same screen, the same misregistration and the same boil as everything else.

Every frame is a separate print: another sheet, another registration, a drum running a little dry. Show on every 2 = Each print is held for two frames, like animation on twos. Video only.
