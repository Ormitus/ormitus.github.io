Brand Identity & Print

Bananhot Sunny City

Visual identity for Bananhot’s “Sunny City” pop-up, created with Persona Studio. The work includes the logo, colour palette, a postcard series and an illustrated visitor map.

Identity System

Logo Design

The logo combines a palm tree with an arched “SUNNY CITY” title and a bold serif BANANHOT wordmark.

Sketches explore the palm tree’s shape and its balance with the wordmark before the final vector version.

Identity & print

Postcard Series

Print Design

Five postcards for visitors to collect at the pop-up.

The postcards pair editorial photography with large BANANHOT type.

Bananhot Swimwear

About the Project

Bananhot is an Israeli swimwear brand by Neta Alchemister. The pop-up takes the form of a fictional resort city, with its own signs, postcards and visitor map.

Ori Mor and Persona Studio collaborated on the graphic design, illustration and print production.
