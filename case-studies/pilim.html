An identity for a parenting centre

Pilim is a parenting centre in Ramat Gan. The identity covers activities for parents, children and extended families, both at the centre and online.

The main symbol combines a parent and child. We adapted its contours and colours to distinguish the centre’s activities.

Process / Before the symbol

Naming explorations

The naming explorations considered animal characters that could suggest warmth and support while addressing parents.

Naming exploration / Elephant

Symbol development

Seven original studies explore contour, negative space and the relationship between two figures.

The final mark brings the relationship into the shape itself: a larger parent form surrounds a smaller child, with two colours keeping both figures visible.

A graphic library from the symbol

Typography / Mandatory Variable

Hebrew and Latin typography

Mandatory Variable carries the display typography in Hebrew and Latin. Its weight and width variations let the identity move from a strong headline to a more compact line, while keeping the same voice.

03 / Brand architecture

Signs for the centre’s activities

Each activity has its own sign, adapted from the elephant mark. The variations use scale, colour and additional shapes to identify the audience or format.

Online meetings

Online meetings

A camera-shaped elephant and a blue accent identify the online meetings, connecting the digital space to the same parent-and-child relationship.

Parents of teenagers

Parents of older children

The child grows almost as tall as the parent. The relationship stays recognisable while the change in scale acknowledges a new stage of parenting.

Grandparents

Grandparents

Three nested figures bring the extended family into one symbol. Orange distinguishes the grandparents, around the parent and child.

In the community

Community activities

Three elephants form a wider circle. Pink identifies community activity and encounters that extend beyond the immediate family.

At the centre

The physical centre

A purple roof turns the elephant into a house: the physical centre where the different activities and audiences meet.

The elephant in the room

The podcast

The centre’s podcast has an independent microphone mark. Its name, “The elephant in the room”, keeps the connection to Pilim without repeating the main brand name.

Brand family / In use

Podcast and online workshops

A podcast cover and a Zoom workshop announcement show two applications of the activity signs.

04 / Out in the world

Posters and announcements

Poster and social formats use the same shapes and colour palette, with the information arranged for each format.

05 / Spatial language

The identity in the space

Spatial visualisations show the shapes applied to walls, partitions and activity spaces.
