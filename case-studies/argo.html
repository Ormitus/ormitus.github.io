Overview

The product concept

Plan a move, compare movers and follow the booking.

ARGO is an iOS app concept for arranging a move. The project covers the journey from entering move details and comparing movers to inventory, booking and moving-day updates.

The proposed matching flow accounts for heavy loads, crane services and special items alongside the move’s location and date.

Problem Identification

Problems in the moving process

Pricing uncertainty, coordination and moving-day stress.

Uncertainty

It's hard to compare prices and services, and the final price can change on moving day. Quotes are given over the phone based on rough estimates — "approximately what's in the apartment" — with no way to contest it.

Emotional Overwhelm

Alongside the practical arrangements, customers worry about timing, damaged items and whether anything will be left behind. The interface needs to keep them informed during the move.

Fragmented Platforms

Customers coordinate across calls, WhatsApp and Facebook, often sending the same photos to several movers. The project explores a shared inventory within the app.

User Research

User profiles

Three personas from the project research.

The Student Moving to the City

Doesn't have a lot of time and is looking for a solution that makes the move easier. Doesn't have much money and wants the best possible quote. Prioritizes speed and transparency above everything else.

The Young Couple

Their schedule is packed, and they need a fast, simple, and convenient solution. No time for delays — they prefer to pay someone to handle everything end-to-end. Values reliability and seamless communication throughout.

The Established Family

Looking for a service that gives them peace of mind and can handle any possible challenge. They have a lot of valuable, sensitive, and large items. Prioritizes trust, safety, and a truly full-service experience.

02 / Home scan

Home inventory

Disorder & Scattered Communication

"I had to send photos of every piece of furniture to each mover separately. Everything was in WhatsApp and Facebook, across multiple parallel conversations. Exhausting and time-consuming."

The home-scanning concept creates an inventory that users can review and share with the movers they contact.

View original flow in Figma
03 / Quote & booking

Itemised quotes

The Price Always Changes on Moving Day

"The quote changed on the actual day of the move. The price was set over the phone based on a rough estimate of 'approximately what's in the apartment.' There was no way to dispute it."

The quote screen links the price to the inventory, with a breakdown by item category for review before booking.

View original flow in Figma
04 / Moving day

Moving-day updates

Emotional Overwhelm on the Day Itself

"Moving day is emotionally charged — enormous stress, fear, and uncertainty. How long will this take? Where are the movers right now? Did they take all my things? What if something breaks?"

The moving-day screens show status updates and mover location. Checklists help users keep track of items and outstanding tasks.

Flow Mapping

Where does ARGO fit in?

Mapping the complete user journey — from first search to move completion

Enter Initial Details & Find a Mover

The user enters move details first, and receives a list of only the movers who are available and work in their area — eliminating long calls that go nowhere before any real information is exchanged.

Scan the Home

Scan the home's contents and create a comprehensive list of the items being moved. Save time and effort — one scan generates an accurate, itemized inventory. No more sending photos over WhatsApp to multiple movers in parallel.

Receive Accurate Quotes & Choose a Mover

Receive precise price quotes from multiple movers and choose the best fit. Transparent pricing breakdown — you know exactly what to expect, with no surprises and no hidden costs.

Book the Move

Select your preferred move time and enter payment details. Simple, secure checkout that locks in the confirmed price before moving day arrives.

Moving Day — Real-Time Updates

Receive updates on the progress of the move and track in real time. Stay informed throughout the day: where are the movers now, what's been loaded, and when will they arrive at the new address?

The Solution

Why download ARGO?

The solutions the app provides — what makes ARGO worth downloading

Feature 01

Transparent Pricing

You know exactly what to expect — no surprises, no hidden costs. Fair quotes without the last-minute price changes that plague traditional moves. 27% cheaper on average for large furniture compared to competitors.

Feature 02

Home Scanning

Save time and energy by scanning your home and receiving an accurate, itemized price estimate. No more sending photos over WhatsApp to multiple movers in parallel — one scan, one inventory, shared automatically.

Feature 03

Continuous Mover Communication

Receive ongoing updates, full transparency, and confidence throughout the entire moving process. Real-time tracking on moving day — know where your movers are, what's been loaded, and when to expect arrival.

Feature 04

Everything in One Place

Consolidate the entire moving process under one platform for efficient, convenient management. Moving checklists, mover chat, booking confirmation, and real-time tracking — all unified in a single app.

Feature 05

Additional Moving Services

Find extra services for your move: professional packing, home organization, purchasing moving supplies, crane and heavy lift services. We unpack, organize, and fit the space so you enjoy your new home from day one.

Feature 06

Moving Day Checklist

Organized checklists so nothing gets forgotten. Stay updated throughout moving day — from pickup to delivery, with everything tracked and confirmed. All your move management in one organized place.
