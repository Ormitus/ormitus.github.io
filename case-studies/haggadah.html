The Project

The Asufa Haggadah

I designed a double-page spread for the 13th edition of the Asufa Passover Haggadah. The annual project invites designers from across Israel to each interpret a passage of the traditional text.

My assigned passage was “Blood, fire, and pillars of smoke” from the Seder text.

The Tradition

Collage and typography

Each spread in the book has its own visual language, bringing the designers’ interpretations together around the same traditional text.

For this passage, I used collage and stencil-like lettering, drawing on the texture and overlapping layers of a protest wall.
