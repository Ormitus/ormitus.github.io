A smash-burger identity, from the counter to clothing.

TIMMY is a smash-burger spot with a small menu. The identity uses a lowercase wordmark, orange and cream, with oversized type across the storefront, packaging and clothing.

The brief called for a clean, mature identity for a neighbourhood restaurant. Vintage diner references informed the typography and the use of stainless steel and cream.

Process / The starting point

The brief and two proposed directions

The client wanted quick service, a focused menu and an identity that would also work on clothing. We explored a typographic direction in orange and a mascot-led direction in green.

Product

Smash. A small menu.

Place

A neighbourhood counter.

Character

Confident and approachable.
Typography before decoration.

Two proposed directions

We presented two directions: orange and green. The orange direction was selected.

Orange / Selected

A typographic label.

Warm orange, cream and confident type. The direction developed into the final identity.

Green / Not selected

A character-led world.

Deep green, cream and a retro mascot, explored across signage, clothing and the menu.

01 / Identity

The selected identity

The lowercase logotype appears on bags, labels and the storefront. Orange is the main brand colour, with cream backgrounds around the mark.

02 / Graphic language

Typography across the brand

We repeated menu words to create patterns and compositions for bags, windows and T-shirts.
