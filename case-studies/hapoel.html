Official fan shop
Merchandise & retail products

As a Visual Designer for Hapoel Tel Aviv FC, I drive the creative vision and visual identity for the club's official fan shop and merchandise line.

I am responsible for the end-to-end design of all retail products, translating the rich heritage, energy, and passionate spirit of the team into compelling apparel and accessories.

From initial concept to final production, I ensure that every item not only resonates deeply with our dedicated fanbase but also elevates the club's brand presence both on and off the pitch.
