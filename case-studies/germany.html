Logo Design  ·  Competition Winner

Israel–Germany, 60 years

Israel — Germany  ·  1965–2025

Logo designed to mark 60 years of diplomatic relations between Israel and Germany — chosen through a dedicated competition and selected as the official emblem for all joint activities throughout the milestone year.

The Concept

Designing the anniversary mark

The mark combines the number 60 with a folded ribbon, using Israel’s blue and Germany’s black, red and gold.

The ribbon also refers to solidarity with the hostages, inspired by gestures of support from German football fans. It connects the anniversary to a humanitarian message.

Germany

Black, Red, Gold — Representing unity, freedom, and strength through the German tricolor.

Israel

Blue and White — The Star of David embedded in the numeral, representing the Israeli identity at the heart of the mark.

The Ribbon

The ribbon is a universal symbol of solidarity — here it simultaneously forms "60" and expresses the bond between two peoples.

Final Mark

The Official Emblem

Selected as the official logo for all joint Israeli-German activities in 2025.
